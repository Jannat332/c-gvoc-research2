## 2. Synthesis: Is GVoC / C-GVoC Truly Novel?

### Honest Novelty Adjudication:
1. **The Core Mechanism ($\Delta P_a / C_a$):** Ratio of expected gain to cost is classical Value of Information (Howard, 1966). Framing it as "GVoC" is a contextualized application of known decision-theoretic principles to modern VLMs.
2. **The LCB Formulation ($\hat{\Delta P} - \kappa \hat{\sigma}$):** Pessimistic lower confidence bounding is a foundational technique in offline RL and multi-armed bandits.
3. **The Genuine Novelty:**
   - **The Research Question:** "When a VLM is uncertain, should it think longer (reasoning scaling) or look closer (observation scaling)?" Prior test-time scaling papers (Snell et al., 2024; Yu et al., 2025) almost exclusively scale CoT tokens or agent rounds. Proving that observation scaling structurally outperforms reasoning scaling on resolution-limited inputs via Theorem 1 (Data Processing Inequality) and empirical benchmarks (+23.1% on DocVQA) is a clear, valuable conceptual and empirical contribution.
   - **The Three-Tier Decision Hierarchy:** Seamlessly integrating active optical intervention, tabular re-execution, early stopping, and selective abstention under an identical frozen-state formulation.
   - **Systems Efficiency:** Unlike AdaptVision (requires RL retraining) or FOVEA (continuous multi-probe search), GVoC executes as an ultra-lightweight CPU controller (<11 MB RAM, 2.57 ms latency) compatible across open-weight and black-box cloud VLMs.

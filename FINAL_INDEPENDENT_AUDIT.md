## 1. Executive Summary & Verification Context

This independent audit evaluated the completed revision of *Think Longer or Look Closer? Test-Time Scaling for Visual Document Understanding* following a comprehensive second-round review addressing simulation transparency, diagnostic claim calibration, visual legibility, technical jargon demystification, and citation coverage.

### Important Audit Distinction on Reproduction Scope
> [!NOTE]
> **Provenance & Reproduction Status:** The underlying database records (`gvoc_evaluation.db`, `live_evaluation.db`, `diagnostic_evaluation.db`, `confirmatory_evaluation.db`) and evaluation scripts were inspected and recomputed via independent statistical verification scripts. However, per research protocol and budget constraints, paid API experiments were not re-executed against live commercial APIs. All reproduction claims reflect **provisional verification from frozen artifact records and source code**, not live re-runs of external model inference.

### Core Audit Outcomes
1. **Simulation Transparency & Table 2 Renaming:** Table 2 is explicitly titled **"Live Pilot Results and Offline Policy-Simulation Results"**. The table caption includes an unambiguous disclosure:
   > *"Simulation results are generated by a model of intervention outcomes under synthetic degradation; they are not accuracy measurements from 16,000 live VLM evaluations."*  
   Furthermore, Section 4.2 provides a transparent explanation for the sharp baseline discrepancy: the offline simulation models severe synthetic downsampling where unassisted zero-shot collapses (12.5%) due to optical erasure, whereas the live pilot evaluates genuine high-resolution documents where direct zero-shot already succeeds at 82.0%, making intervention overhead counterproductive.
2. **Properly Calibrated Diagnostic Claims ($N=12$ and $N=20$):**
   - **Supported:** Concise single-turn prompting eliminated prompt truncation ($p = 0.0078$, Fisher exact).
   - **Suggestive, Non-Significant:** The apparent accuracy shift from 58.3% (7/12) to 83.3% (10/12) in the prompt probe is explicitly reported as non-significant ($p = 0.3750$). Truncation elimination is properly framed as a format fix, not proven accuracy superiority.
   - **Exploratory:** Center cropping collapsed accuracy to 25.0% (5/20) vs. 60.0% downsampled, with raw $p = 0.0391$. The manuscript explicitly discloses that this comparison does not survive Bonferroni multiplicity adjustment ($p_{\text{adj}} = 0.1563$) and that all 20 probe queries completely overlap with the 100-document live pilot.
   - **Causal Humility:** Probes are explicitly described as providing suggestive failure mechanisms rather than definitive causal proofs for the live-pilot performance gap.
3. **Redesigned, High-Legibility Visuals:**
   - **Figure 2 (Page 2):** Redesigned and simplified into three bold, uncluttered stages (*Stage 1 Diagnostic Signals* $\to$ *Stage 2 Value-of-Compute Gate* $\to$ *Stage 3 Targeted Execution*). Font sizes were increased to 11–13pt with minimal internal text, ensuring immediate readability at publication print scale.
   - **Figure 3 (Page 4):** Dual-panel Pareto chart redesigned with enlarged fonts (10pt axis labels, 10.5pt titles, 9–9.5pt data callouts), prominent markers ($s = 140–240$), uncluttered annotations, and an explicit caption identifying live vs. simulated takeaways.
4. **Jargon Demystified in Plain Academic Prose:**
   - *Counterfactual intervention value / same-state branching:* Explained at first use as evaluating what would have happened by testing alternative actions from the identical intermediate point, isolating action effects from trajectory differences.
   - *Telemetry dispersion $\hat{\sigma}$:* Defined as an uncertainty dispersion proxy measuring variance across visual and textual features.
   - *Oracle regret:* Defined as the gap between a policy's return and the best available candidate action.
   - *Value-of-compute gate:* Translated into plain language: *"Even after penalizing for uncertainty, does the conservative improvement from looking closer still justify the tokens spent?"*
5. **Expanded, 100% Verified Bibliography:** The bibliography in `naacl/custom.bib` has been expanded from 18 to **31 authentic peer-reviewed citations**, covering test-time compute allocation, active perception, value of information, LayoutLM document architectures, and established visual document benchmarks. Zero fictitious or hallucinated citations remain.
6. **Strict Venue Page-Budget Adherence:**
   - **Main Text:** Strictly **4 pages** (Pages 1 to 4, concluding with Section 8 at the bottom of Page 4).
   - **References:** Begins cleanly at the top of **Page 5** and spans Pages 5–6.
   - **Appendices:** Pages 7–12 (Formal proofs, information theory, telemetry specification, hardware details, and full contingency tables).
   - **Double-Blind Anonymity:** 100% compliant (`\author{Anonymous Authors}`, zero unblinded URLs).
